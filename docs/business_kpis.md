# Business KPI Layer

## Objective

Provide business-ready metrics for reporting and dashboarding.

---

## KPI Executive Summary

Provides:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Average Delivery Days
- Late Delivery Percentage

---

## KPI Sales By State

Measures:

- Revenue by State
- Orders by State

Business Use:

- Regional Performance Analysis
- Geographic Sales Distribution

---

## KPI Sales By Category

Measures:

- Revenue by Product Category
- Orders by Product Category

Business Use:

- Product Performance Analysis
- Category Ranking

---

## KPI Monthly Sales Trend

Measures:

- Monthly Revenue

Business Use:

- Trend Analysis
- Seasonality Detection
- Growth Monitoring

---

## KPI Delivery Performance

Measures:

- On-Time Deliveries
- Late Deliveries

Business Use:

- Logistics Performance
- Customer Experience Monitoring
