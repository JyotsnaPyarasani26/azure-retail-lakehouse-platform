# Business Requirements

## Objective

Building a Retail Lakehouse Platform using Databricks, Delta Lake, PySpark and SQL to analyze e-commerce sales performance.

---

## Business Questions

1. What are the top-selling product categories?

2. Which sellers generate the highest revenue?

3. What are the monthly sales trends?

4. Which states have the highest customer purchases?

5. What is the average delivery time?

6. What percentage of orders are delivered late?

7. Which payment methods are most frequently used?

8. What is the customer review score distribution?

---

## Expected Deliverables

### Bronze Layer
Raw ingestion of source files.

### Silver Layer
Cleaned and standardized business entities.

### Gold Layer
Star schema optimized for reporting.

### Dashboard Layer
Executive KPI dashboard in Power BI.
